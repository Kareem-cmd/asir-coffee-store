# بن عسير

## Platform
Arabic RTL responsive web storefront.

## Stack
Delegated by the user: dependency-free HTML, CSS and JavaScript. GitHub Pages hosting requested. Browser-local demo cart and checkout; no payment or server collection.

## Product purpose
Demonstrate a coffee shop using the supplied Asir Coffee Association identity, banners, icons, product photography and additional products extracted from its brand guide.

## Confirmed constraints
Preserve original branding and artwork. Original source folder remains untouched. Publish implementation to the user's GitHub account. Names, prices, product variants and descriptions beyond visible packaging are demonstration assumptions, clearly disclosed. Do not invent testimonials, certifications or delivery guarantees.

## Audience assumptions
Arabic-speaking coffee shoppers in Saudi Arabia, shopping on mobile and desktop; currency SAR.
